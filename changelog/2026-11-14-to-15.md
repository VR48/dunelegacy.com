# Dune City 1.0.867 to 1.0.871

Four releases over two days took Dune City from 1.0.865 to 1.0.871. Versions 1.0.866 and 1.0.868 were built but never published. This summary describes the game as it stands in 1.0.871. Each release's own notes are below it.

## Campaign AI opponent

- The **Campaign AI** opponent follows the original Dune II campaign rules in new games. An enemy house stays idle until one of your units has seen one of its ground units. Its factories choose what to build the original way. It attacks with newly built units and reinforcements one or two at a time, not massed groups.
- Each Campaign AI house keeps its mission's unit limit (15 to 25 units), unless you set your own maximum. Destroyed buildings are rebuilt from up to five remembered slots. The Death Hand aims at the first enemy building. Damage to its base no longer sends its whole army out.
- 1.0.869 made this opponent far too hard from level 6, and 1.0.870 fixed that. In 264 Full campaign test games, with an idle human and a Hard QuantBot helper standing in for the player, levels 1 to 5 were won 78 of 78 times. Levels 6 to 9 were won 51 of 54 times, against 49 of 54 in 1.0.867. Nobody has yet confirmed this by playing the missions.
- Games saved before 1.0.869 keep the earlier Campaign AI behaviour when loaded. QuantBot is a separate choice.

## QuantBot campaign opponents

- Easy, Medium and Hard opponents now differ in clear steps. They keep at most 1, 2 and 4 harvesters and send attack waves worth 1,700, 2,500 and 3,500. A Hard opponent waits one more minute after the map's first enemy reinforcement before its first attack. Brutal opponents are unchanged.
- Only Easy opponents are denied a Repair Yard. Medium QuantBots in Custom games no longer inherit that ban.
- Campaign QuantBots no longer upgrade their Construction Yard straight away. They wait until they have income and the power they need, and can keep 2,000 credits in reserve.
- The campaign setup screen now describes the opponent levels correctly.

## QuantBot campaign helper

These apply to the QuantBot that plays alongside you in the classic campaign.

- **It holds its ground.** Troops that are not out on an attack no longer chase targets more than 14 tiles from your nearest building.
- **It waits for a strong army.** It holds its attacks until its army matches the strongest enemy alliance, instead of sending small waves one at a time.
- **It builds a bigger economy.** With a Starport available it builds six refineries before the Starport and eight once the Repair Yard is up, with one carryall for every two harvesters.
- **It saves on concrete and tanks.** It lays concrete only under its factories. It builds the Repair Yard before its first Heavy Factory and buys early tanks from the Starport.
- It builds a Repair Yard at every difficulty. On spice-quota missions a helper that shares your house stops spending once half the quota is banked.
- On missions 20 to 22 against Hard QuantBot opponents, 216 test games for each helper level, a Brutal helper won 129 against 74 in 1.0.869. A Hard helper won 121 (95 before), a Medium helper 81 (41) and an Easy helper 15 (3).

## Controls

- **The classic command keys are the defaults:** A Attack, S Stop, M Move, Q Patrol, H Hold, C Capture, D Carryall, R Repair, U Upgrade, P Place, G next Construction Yard and F next factory.
- **Every unit command has a key:** E Guard, X Area Guard, B Ambush, N Hunt, K Retreat, Y Deploy MCV, V movement paths and Delete for a Devastator's self-destruct.
- **Options > Hotkeys** is new: any of the 33 commands can be given a different key.
- **W, A, S and D scroll the map only if you switch on "WASD camera"** under Options > Controls. It is off by default.

## Also new

- Long matches with large armies use less AI time.
- The city graphics skin is called Classic, and the bundled two-player city map is "2P - 192x192 - City".
- The About screen says Dune City is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts, Westwood Studios, the Herbert estate or any Dune licensee.

## Known issues

- Late missions against Hard QuantBot opponents are still lost about four times in ten with a Brutal helper and an idle human. The weakest are Atreides mission 20 and Harkonnen mission 21.
- Against Brutal QuantBot opponents the helper lost all 216 late-mission test games.
- The new keys have not been tried on German or French keyboard layouts.

## Compatibility

- Multiplayer uses **protocol 126**: all players should run the same version.
- Saves use format **9886**. Saves from earlier versions still load.
- Android has a separate version and publication process.
