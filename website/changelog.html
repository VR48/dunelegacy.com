What's new in Dune City

Every desktop release, newest first. The game offers each new release on the main menu; saves, settings and your own mods carry over.

Recent releases have written notes. For older releases without notes, the list shows the developers' change summaries. "All code changes" opens the full comparison on GitHub.

Dune City 1.0.850 21 October 2026

  • Faster big games: large maps with many houses run about twice as fast. An 8-house 384×384 game went from about 19 to 42 fps on a MacBook Air in Low Power Mode. When the computer can't keep up, single-player games now slow down slightly instead of stuttering.
  • The AI attacks in free-for-alls: QuantBot armies no longer sit in their bases when several houses play. A house now judges whether it is outnumbered against its strongest single enemy, gathers outside its own city, and a leading house goes after the weakest one. Games end with a winner far more often.
  • Difficulty levels: Brutal > Hard > Medium > Easy now holds in long games. Medium attacks with its whole army, and Brutal builds a bigger economy using its higher harvester and army limits. No difficulty cheats on resources.
  • Unit mixes: each house starts with a tuned unit mix (Atreides, Harkonnen, Ordos, Fremen) and a mix designed for each tech level, e.g. tank-heavy at tech 4. No unit type starts above 50% or below 2%, and the AI still adapts during the game.
  • Campaign: the AI partner wins most late missions, and DuneCity missions that never finished now end. City tax credits count towards a spice quota.
  • Fixed: missions in the DuneCity mod were won as soon as the city reached 500 people.
  • Fixed: the Attack button or A key on a spice bloom now shoots the bloom from range instead of driving onto it.
  • Removed: sharing maps to the online catalogue and browsing it. Hosting a game with your own mod still works.
  • Saves from 1.0.842 still load. Multiplayer needs every player on 1.0.850.

Dune City 1.0.842 15 October 2026

  • Unit orders: attack-move (A), patrol (Q), Hold position, Shift-queued waypoints and repeat production.
  • Formations: select two or more ground units and choose Box, Pincer, Column, Staggered or Echelon in the unit panel (Ctrl+F on/off, Ctrl+Shift+F cycles). The units form up where they stand, then move, attack-move and patrol as one group at one speed. Siege tanks lead, harvesters stay out, an MCV takes the centre, and nothing stands in a sonic tank's line of fire.
  • Plan building sites: queue buildings and place them in advance; they are built on the plan when ready, spread over all Construction Yards. This is a game rule (Game Rules: "Plan building sites when ordering").
  • Random Skirmish: generated maps with natural terrain, clustered bases, zoned cities and nuclear power. Choose your start, an AI partner, 0-6 allies and 1-7 enemies, rock up to 100%, any installed mod and the game rules. Random army and city sizes are the same for every house.
  • Multiplayer random maps: hosts choose "Random map..." in the map chooser, set the size, terrain and what every house starts with, then players join and set houses and teams in the normal lobby.
  • Sidebar: the build list is a two-column grid, so a full Construction Yard fits without scrolling.
  • QuantBot: escorts a moving MCV in a ring. Its attack waves keep the usual behaviour: no formation won more in 1,980 test games.
  • Saves from 1.0.833 still load. Multiplayer needs every player on 1.0.842.

Dune City 1.0.833 13 October 2026

A robustness release. Every test build now runs with memory and undefined-behaviour checking, and the problems it found are fixed.

  • Long games no longer make QuantBot's choice between Devastators, Sonic Tanks and Deviators unreliable. With a lot of combat history the score that ranks them overflowed and could rank them wrongly; it now stays a proper average of what each unit has achieved.
  • Game graphics, animations and mentat texts are read safely regardless of how their data is laid out in the original files.
  • AdLib music is generated the same way on Apple Silicon as on Intel, where negative waveform positions used to be clamped instead of wrapping.
  • Tutorial hints no longer misfire for items whose numbers are above 31.
  • A malformed house number in a multiplayer lobby packet is now rejected cleanly.

Existing saves remain loadable. Multiplayer protocol is 70: all peers must update together. Saves, settings and user maps remain in the separate user folder.

Dune City 1.0.832 11 October 2026

QuantBot armies now fight when their bases are attacked, instead of marching back and forth across the map. On Hard and Brutal, an attack on any building used to send the whole army after whichever enemy fired last, with orders that stopped units shooting on the way; with several colonies under attack at once, launchers and tanks kept turning around and hardly fired.

  • The troops nearest the attacked building now answer it, enough of them to outweigh the attackers, and only from within a bounded distance that grows with the size of the attack.
  • They travel in Hunt mode and attack any enemy they meet on the way, rather than ignoring it until they arrive. They are not sent elsewhere while they are on their way, fighting, or while enemies remain at that building.
  • Units already carrying out another forced order, such as a launcher stepping back to firing range, are left to finish it.

On a reported 3waysplit save, Sardaukar launchers went from 7 shots to 296 over the same two minutes of play.

Existing saves remain loadable. Multiplayer protocol is 69: all peers must update together. Saves, settings and user maps remain in the separate user folder.

Dune City 1.0.831 11 October 2026

QuantBot now spreads its bases across the map. Once its home rock is built out or already holds two construction yards, a new MCV drives to free rock elsewhere and founds a colony there, instead of stacking more yards on the same formation.

  • Colonists travel with an escort. Each MCV heading for new rock waits on its home rock until its three nearest free vehicles have gathered, then drives out with them. Escorts guard the MCV on the way and turn on anything that damages it. A colonist under fire keeps driving instead of stopping in place.
  • An MCV boxed in by buildings with only diagonal exits can now find a way out and deploy. Previously it could stand idle for the rest of the match and block further MCV purchases.
  • Colonising no longer waits for three rocket turrets over every outlying yard. Each new colony still orders its own turret cover once its yard stands.
  • Colonists lost on the way are remembered: two recent losses pause new colonists until they age out, so the AI does not feed MCVs into a contested corridor.
  • QuantBot's internal organisation was restructured with no change to its decisions; seeded AI matches produce identical decision logs before and after.
  • Fixes a crash when starting a spectated game on a map that places a unit where it cannot stand, such as two sandworms on one tile. The unit is now skipped and the match starts.

Validation covered Sihaya-Ferryman Brutal-versus-Brutal matches on three seeds over 50 game minutes, with both houses founding one to three colonies and no MCV left idle at the end.

Existing saves remain loadable. Multiplayer protocol is 68: all peers must update together. Saves, settings and user maps remain in the separate user folder.

Dune City 1.0.829 10 October 2026

Ornithopters now hunt enemy combat units on Hard and Brutal, prioritising nearby launchers when their wing can engage. Easy and Medium defend their own bases and harvesters and raid enemy bases only when the approach and target are clear of observed anti-air. Autonomous ornithopters never hunt harvesters.

  • Aircraft target planning shares bounded caches rather than scanning the world for every aircraft. Launchers regroup with attacking armies and recover from stalled wave orders. QuantBot reserves a mobile anti-air share when hostile aircraft are present and can build Troopers and their required infantry technology.
  • Killing a high-performing enemy unit type earns a larger combat-value bonus. The bonus follows the victim type's measured performance, with bounded rewards and a fallback while evidence is scarce.
  • The new Stats menu shows active units, kills, deaths and damage dealt, plus units produced, spice harvested, tax collected and population. QuantBot performance appears as xN.N, alongside each type's target army-value percentage. Observers can browse every house's Stats and Budget, with the house name visible.
  • 1920 × 1080 and other supported HD render choices now appear on Retina displays. The chosen render resolution remains saved while the window fits the desktop without changing its aspect ratio. Native interface sizing remains available.
  • Direct multiplayer avoids flooding a waiting peer with identical command windows, preserving immediate new-order delivery and periodic retries.
  • Includes airport ambient aircraft and three additional community maps: Reacciona rapido forro, Alto mapa HD and La guerra dei ricchi.

Validation covered 14 bounded AI games across Vanilla, Dune2R, Dune City, campaign and all four difficulties, with three matched 1.0.820 baseline runs. One city-layout case was excluded from city-simulation coverage because it used Vanilla. No crashes or ornithopter harvester targets were recorded. Hard city combat shifts strongly toward aircraft; tested launcher and rocket-turret counters, army-wave progress and city economies remained viable. These runs establish the tested scenarios, not universal player balance.

Existing saves remain loadable. Multiplayer protocol is 67: all peers must update together. Saves, settings and user maps remain in the separate user folder.

Dune City 1.0.812 7 October 2026

The game-speed controls now reach 3 ms and 2 ms per simulation cycle, twice the previous 4 ms maximum. The default stays at 16 ms and the slow end at 32 ms; existing saved speed choices are kept. A controlled 60 Hz measurement reached about 333 and 500 cycles per second at the new settings, but slow rendering, heavy simulation or high latency can hold a match below its configured rate. This is not a frame-rate improvement for large CPU-bound armies.

  • Ground-unit combat rotation now wraps the direction correctly after a movement turn. An invalid direction previously overwrote tile memory, causing late-join crashes and observer divergence on busy maps. Tile track writes also reject invalid directions.
  • Observer checkpoint recovery replaces an open game menu with working join-progress controls and closes it before the match is rebuilt.
  • Custom Hard and Brutal QuantBot sends every available ground combat unit to Hunt in one pass, and a real attack on any owned building — including a remote R/C/I colony — scrambles the ground army to the invasion. Hunting launchers keep their close-range escape and resume firing; reserves assemble at a protected rally outside the colony footprint. Human orders, repair, workers and aircraft keep their existing authority.
  • Bases grow overlapping rocket batteries towards the observed enemy approach. In DuneCity Custom Hard/Brutal games, a battery with no free site can reclaim an owned residential, commercial or industrial lot on the enemy-facing side. Free ground always wins, and local lot minimums, hospitals, churches, roads, factory exits and corridors are preserved.
  • Crowded armies waste less pathfinding work and keep valid cached routes. A ground unit stalled on one tile for 30 seconds with an outstanding movement order can request an owned free carryall.
  • Carryall pickup no longer loses its passenger, and bookings are released on both sides. Units orphaned by that fault in older saves return to the map, so hidden units can no longer keep a match from ending.
  • Police Stations no longer claim "Unit limit reached" below the game's own unit limit. They use the house's existing Trike and Trooper admission rules, and Brutal's separate military-value gate now reads "Army target reached".
  • Custom Game offers a per-house spice income multiplier of 1x to 5x for players and bots, explained above the roster: it multiplies credits from harvester deliveries, with 1x the original rate. The "Original" house-colour selector and its bonus-palette checkbox were removed; saved colour choices remain intact.

Existing saves remain loadable, and saves written before these changes default to a 1x spice rate. Save format is 9852. Multiplayer protocol is 57, which excludes peers on older builds: all players must update together before playing online. Saves, settings and user mods remain in the separate user folder.
