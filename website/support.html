          Questions

          What is public?

          Your alias, your tier and month, and for features the title, description and optional GitHub username. Your PayPal email, name and wallet address are not stored or shown. See the privacy page.

          Is the yearly membership a subscription?

          No. Each donation is a single payment and nothing renews automatically. Your alias stays on the wall for 12 months from the payment.

          My crypto payment is not confirmed yet

          Keep the page open: it checks again every 15 seconds until the network has confirmed the transaction (about a minute on Base, a few minutes on Ethereum). If you closed the page, enter the same details and transaction hash again.

          Something went wrong

          Ask on Discord with your PayPal receipt or transaction hash and we will sort it out.
