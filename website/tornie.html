TORNIE MOD

More houses. More units. New campaigns.

Tornie is a content mod by Tornie_Panther. It adds factions, units, structures, spice types and campaigns for every house. Since Dune City 1.0.863 it matches Tornie 1.0.546, and its sister mods Tornie Lite, Jericho, Jericho Lite, ArcaKev and ArcaKev Lite ship too.

Tornie's website Play in Dune City

Tornie's guide

Tornie publishes a full guide to the mod: units, buildings, technologies, factions and modes, achievements, a player guide and a picture gallery, in English and French.

The standalone Dune Legacy Tornie edition for Windows and Linux is released on Tornie's GitHub releases.

Twelve factions

Fremen, Sardaukar, Mercenary, Neutral, Rebels, Tharpique, Wildspade and Kleshmersh join Atreides, Ordos and Harkonnen, each with its own campaign. Lite editions play with six houses.

Units and rules

Flame Quad, Gas Infantry, Elite Ornithopter, Slow Tank, Tame Sandworm, the two-bay Doublefinery and more. Sell buildings, research Radar upgrades and try Chaos Mode.

Faction co-op

Play a campaign as up to four allied houses with two players each, plus Easy Mode, coloured spice sets and 73 achievements.

Play Tornie in Dune City

The Tornie mods ship with every Dune City release as separately selectable mods. Download Dune City or play in your browser, then choose Tornie (or another edition) in the Mod list when you pick your house for a Campaign or set up a Custom Game. The map editor asks for a mod too, so you can build Tornie maps.

Tornie's content and rules only apply while one of these mods is active. Switching back to Vanilla, Dune City or Dune2R removes them again; your saves and settings stay where they are.

For online games, every player needs the same Dune City release and the same mod. The standalone Dune Legacy Tornie edition is a separate game and cannot join Dune City lobbies.
